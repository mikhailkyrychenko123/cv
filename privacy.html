Privacy notice

For contact forms, analytics and the visitor counter · Updated 27 November 2026

This notice explains how information submitted through the Rusty Drop shop reservation form and handmade product inquiry form is used, and how website analytics and the site's small visitor counter work.

Who is responsible

Mikhail K., based in Kyiv, Ukraine, operates this form. For privacy questions or requests, email mikhail.kyrychenko@gmail.com.

What is collected and why

The Rusty Drop form collects your email address, the time you gave consent, and the version of this notice shown to you. The product inquiry form collects your email address, the product you asked about, the time you gave consent, and the notice version. These details are used only to respond to the request you submitted. The legal basis is your consent. Neither form adds you to a marketing newsletter.

Storage and access

Submissions are stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database. Only the site operator can view or export them through the password-protected admin page. Cloudflare provides hosting and database services and may process data on its global network. Where required, cross-border transfers are covered by Cloudflare’s Data Processing Addendum. Cloudflare may also process technical request information to operate and secure its network; see its privacy policy.

How long data is kept

Reservation details are kept while the first-shop campaign is open, unless you withdraw consent sooner; the list is deleted when the campaign closes. Product inquiry details are kept only while a response and related follow-up are needed, then deleted. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing the address above; the related entry will then be removed.

Visitor counter

The footer counter shows aggregate page views, daily unique browsers, and the number of browsers that recently sent a short activity signal. It counts pages on this site and uses Kyiv local dates for “today” and “yesterday.” If your browser has Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control enabled, the counter does not record your visit. Otherwise, a random identifier is kept in your browser's local storage and a one-way hash is used to count one unique browser per day; it is not linked to form submissions. The counter does not ask for or store your email, name, IP address, or location. Daily counter records are kept for 35 days; recent-activity records are removed after 24 hours.

Website analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and improve the website. Google Analytics may process page addresses, referral information, browser and device details, approximate location, and interaction events. It may use cookies or similar technologies in your browser. The analytics tag does not load when your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. Google provides the analytics service and processes this information under its Privacy Policy. Analytics is separate from the site's contact forms and visitor counter.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your record, or withdraw consent. Where data-protection law applies, you may also have the right to restrict processing or complain to your local supervisory authority. To make a request, email mikhail.kyrychenko@gmail.com.
